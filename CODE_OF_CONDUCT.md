# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want RunnerTray to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Good behaviour includes:

- Being kind and patient, and assuming good intent.
- Respecting different opinions and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.

Not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Insults, personal or political attacks, and trolling.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when
someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/azharudh33n/runner-tray/security/advisories/new)
(choose it even for conduct reports; it is the private channel GitHub provides), or by contacting
the maintainer on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that break this Code of
Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor.
